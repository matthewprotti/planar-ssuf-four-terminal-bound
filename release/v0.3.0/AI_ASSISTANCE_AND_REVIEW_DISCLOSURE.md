# AI assistance and review disclosure

Matthew Protti directed the research program, selected the problems, supplied
human judgment and release decisions, and is responsible for the public
claims. AI systems were used extensively for conjecture generation, symbolic
and exact computation, proof drafting, adversarial checking, packaging, and
reproducibility engineering. Some claim-scoped reviews were performed by
humans using AI systems as active mathematical tools. Those are described as
**human-directed AI-assisted reviews**, not as conventional journal peer
review and not as unaided human derivations.

The release preserves exact source packages, hashes, replay scripts, evidence
labels, and stated review limitations so readers can distinguish discovery,
verification, blind reproduction, adversarial review, and conventional peer
review.
