# v0.3.0 — fixed-gadget scenario-cover program

Released 4 August 2026 from the immutable `v0.2.1` baseline commit
`102988d5f4025b2ae081aea7fbed0e16fcc7de2a`.

This unrefereed research release adds the arbitrary/legal one-scenario theorem,
the positive scenario-count ladder, scenario-cover duality and its exact fixed
finite atlas, the narrow SC-006 fixed-family phase theorem, and the repaired
high-heterogeneity tail for the same four-terminal gadget.

For coordinatewise positive scenarios, the released fixed-gadget ladder is

$$
\beta_G^{(m,+)}=
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{299-41\sqrt{41}}{32},&m=1,\\[1mm]
\dfrac{17}{8},&m=2,\\[1mm]
3,&m=3,\\
4,&m\ge4.
\end{cases}
$$

The signed-and-zero extension is one-scenario only. The two-, three-, and
four-or-more-scenario values are non-attained suprema; no attainment posture is
claimed for the one-scenario value.

## Scope and nonclaims

Every new global optimization is over legal normalized instances on the one
released fixed gadget. This release does not claim unrestricted planar
sharpness, topology-wide optimality, a signed/zero multi-scenario extension, a
solution of the global bounded-heterogeneity middle region, or the exact F064
value.

## Evidence and review

The human-readable analytic proofs are authoritative. Exact certificates,
deterministic replay, symbolic checks, mutation tests, a blind independent
reconstruction of the fixed finite atlas, and claim-scoped adversarial reviews
provide narrower corroboration. One supplied external human report is
scope-limited and does not cover the complete SC-006 or GM-008/009 proofs.
Nothing in this record is represented as conventional journal peer review,
formal proof-assistant verification, or exhaustive novelty clearance.

The full proof map, claim ledger, review labels, limitations, and source hashes
are included in the repository. The reviewed controlling proof-source bytes
were preserved during final publication editing.
